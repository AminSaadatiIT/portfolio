Frequently Asked Questions

How fast do you respond to inquiries?
I reply to all messages within 24 hours, usually much faster during business hours. For urgent projects, call directly.
Do you work outside Mashhad?
Yes. I've delivered projects across Iran including Tehran, Isfahan, and Ahvaz. Travel costs are included transparently in the quote.

Always. Every cabling link is certified with printed reports, fiber links come with OTDR traces, and you receive full as-built documentation.

Yes. Every project includes a support period. If anything related to my installation fails, I fix it. That's why my callback rate is effectively zero.

Absolutely. I regularly collaborate with in-house IT teams and other contractors — clear scope division keeps everything smooth.
